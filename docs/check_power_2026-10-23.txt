Car on a box, wheels in the air, one pack of 6 alkaline AA cells feeding everything.
The script reads the lowest 5 V the Arduino measured during each step (heartbeat "H,mV").

  motors OFF    lowest 5 V: 5.18 V
  motors HALF   lowest 5 V: 4.11 V
  motors FULL   lowest 5 V: 5.18 V
  servo: answered in 5 of 5 directions

Values copied from my terminal output. In the HALF and FULL steps the motors made a whining
sound and only one motor turned slightly, so the FULL reading is not a real full-load test.

problem seen while driving (the sensor stopped sweeping).
